# Avatar Art Guide

Maintain a consistency sheet and `visualIdentityKey` for every fictional face across all era variants. Track each asset as PLACEHOLDER, GENERATED, APPROVED, or FINAL.

- Original fictional subjects; no celebrity faces or historical-person lookalikes.
- Editorial realism and historical financial portrait photography, never comic, anime, cartoon, or cyberpunk.
- Dark neutral background, consistent soft directional lighting, chest-up pose, centered eyes, restrained era cues.
- Master crop 4:5, sufficient margin for 1:1 profile crops, WebP delivery.
- Prepare neutral, focused, concerned, and crisis treatments through styling before commissioning separate images.
