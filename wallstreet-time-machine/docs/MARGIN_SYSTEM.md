# Margin System

The active HUD continuously displays margin used, margin available, and utilization context. Short tickets preview initial and maintenance requirements before confirmation; COVER and SELL remain visible responses to margin pressure.

The margin account reports equity, cash, restricted proceeds, collateral, long and short market value, used and available margin, initial and maintenance requirements, and utilization. It is recalculated after every price change and order.

Maintenance deficits create a margin call. Players resolve it by covering shorts or selling long assets; no external cash deposit exists. An unresolved call can trigger deterministic forced cover after the active rule's configured delay.
