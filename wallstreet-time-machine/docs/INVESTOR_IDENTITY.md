# Investor Identity

The identity remains stable across eras. Era presentation changes its environment and styling without changing the profile, archetype, abilities, or gameplay values.

Every career owns one `InvestorProfile`: player name, portrait, presentation, archetype, starting strategy, shared career level and XP, traits, perks, mentor, and legacy statistics. Presentation has no mathematical market effect. Existing careers migrate into a required setup state while their capital and history remain intact.
