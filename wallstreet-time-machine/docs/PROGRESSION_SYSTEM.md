# Progression System

Career levels also unlock perk tiers. Era completion may award a trait or mentor; no second XP currency or monetary unlock system exists.

Episode score is centralized and bounded from 0 to 1,000. It combines objective completion (350 points), return (300), drawdown control (250), and strategy discipline (100).

Grades are S at 900, A at 750, B at 600, C at 450, and D below 450. XP is at least 100 and otherwise half the score. Career level is `floor(XP / 1000) + 1`, capped at 10.

The result record includes score, grade, XP, ending capital, return, maximum drawdown, objectives completed, and completion time.
