# Game Feel

Sprint 2.5 presents each deterministic round as a short historical market sequence: date, market movement, news, events, portfolio revaluation, and risk. The calculation completes and persists before presentation begins. Players may reveal all immediately.

The interface combines restrained terminal data, period-sensitive headlines, and crisis emphasis. It avoids continuous flashes, casino feedback, artificial forecasts, and automated investment actions. Market Heat summarizes only currently known sentiment, events, news density, and risk alerts.
